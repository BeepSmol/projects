🌑 ABOUT

Shadow Defense is a co-op multiplayer survival game where you and your team protect a glowing Core from waves of shadow enemies.

You ARE the shadow — position yourself between the Core and the enemies to block their light and destroy them.

Each wave gets harder. Survive as long as you can, upgrade between waves, and don't let the Core's HP hit zero!

🎮 CONTROLS

MouseMove toward cursor
Click + HoldFocus shadow — deals 5× damage
WASD / ArrowsMove (local split-screen)
Space / EnterFocus (WASD / Arrow players)
TOpen chat
TabScoreboard
EscPause menu

⚡ TIPS

▸Stay close to the Core — your shadow cone only blocks enemies in your line-of-sight from it.
▸Hold click / Space to Focus your shadow — smaller cone but 5× damage. Use it when enemies are lined up.
▸Obelisks cast permanent shadows. Place them to cover angles your team can't watch.
▸Boss waves appear every 5 waves — they orbit the Core and take massive HP. Focus them!
▸In multiplayer, spread out and cover all angles around the Core as a team.

🌐 MULTIPLAYER

▸Host clicks HOST SERVER, shares the ID with friends.
▸Clients paste the ID and click CONNECT, then ready up.
▸Max 5 players per lobby.
